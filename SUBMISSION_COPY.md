# VideoDB Submission Copy

**KathaQuest**

KathaQuest turns textbook chapters into short, multilingual video learning journeys for children using real educational footage.

A parent, teacher or child uploads a chapter and selects an age and language. KathaQuest extracts exactly three key concepts and converts them into evidence-focused media queries. VideoDB ingests and indexes a trusted archive of public-domain U.S. Geological Survey videos with spoken-word and visual scene understanding. It retrieves the exact moments that explain each concept and compiles them into playable HLS micro-lessons.

Every episode displays its source, licence, timestamps and relevance. Children can ask questions using text or Hindi voice input; instead of returning only generated text, KathaQuest searches the archive again and answers with a supporting video moment. A three-question quiz can identify weak concepts and create a personalised revision reel.

VideoDB powers archive ingestion, transcript and scene indexing, collection-wide semantic search, timestamp retrieval, evidence metadata and stream compilation. Sarvam AI provides Indian-language speech and narration, while ElevenLabs provides English voice generation with automatic Sarvam fallback. OpenTelemetry and SigNoz trace the complete workflow, including a controlled provider failure and recovery.
